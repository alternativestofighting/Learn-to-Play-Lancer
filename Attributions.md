# ATTRIBUTIONS & LINKS
## Maps:
* RoadEscort.webp - "map_extra_ProtectTheEngineers_v2" map, originally made by awalloftext for the No Room for a Wallflower campaign and distributed in the [Community Wallflower Resources Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dsxLA5G4BfwqygLeHXYNQ76FB7HBhCd7).
* OutpostDefense.webp - "Protect the Engineers (Holdout)" map, originally made by Klondike for the No Room for a Wallflower campaign and distributed in the [Community Wallflower Resources Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dsxLA5G4BfwqygLeHXYNQ76FB7HBhCd7).
## Pilot Portraits:
* All six character portraits are from [Einfly's free Universal Sci-Fi Portrait Packs](https://einflys.itch.io/), available at. Specifically: Marshmallow, Catch, Jetstream, and Djinn are from the basic Portrait Pack, Lodestar is from the Science pack, and Fafnir is from the Piracy pack.
* For the "Comms" versions I use for the LA HUD portraits of pilots while in combat, I modified the aforementioned Einfly portraits in GIMP using Newsprint, Color Balance, Bloom, Video Degradation, and other effects. I followed the steps describe in [this Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/GIMP/comments/1cqn4wi/comment/l3slguy/).
## NPC Tokens:
* Most of the enemies are using tokens from [Artifacter's In Golden Flame chibi token set](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pVEtOvP512eOjqzRJMJFt2VGcv9cg3TF), distributed by ada-at-work's [IGF community resource spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SUZx4YIG9xyEtBwYjZGqicFdYQk05vFzSw_Z97O-fMY).
* Additional enemies are person edits that I made in GIMP of the amazing [Pishly's](https://pishly.itch.io/) free token packs. Specifically, the Goliath, Latch Drone, Support, and Priest tokens use art from [Pishly's GMS Pattern Pack](https://pishly.itch.io/pishly-lancer-tokens).
* There are a few Deployables that use the default art provided by [Retrograde Minis](https://retrogrademinis.com/) for the Lancer system on Foundry.
* The Cargo Truck uses "Big Truck," originally made by Alkyama for the No Room for a Wallflower campaign and distributed in the [Community Wallflower Resources Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dsxLA5G4BfwqygLeHXYNQ76FB7HBhCd7).
